Placeholder Assets for Platformer Demo

This demo uses colored rectangles and circles as placeholder graphics.

When creating your own game, replace these with actual sprite images:
- Player sprite: 32x48px
- Platform tiles: Variable sizes
- Collectibles: 16x16px
- Goal marker: 64x64px

Supported formats: PNG, JPG, BMP, GIF

Place your sprite images in: assets/sprites/
Place your sounds in: assets/sounds/
Place your music in: assets/music/
